Hello, I am a high school senior from Canada. As a kid, I learnt the basics of programming writing Python programs on Windows 7. My first experience with Linux was when I played with the [Kano Computing Kit](https://www.pcmag.com/news/test-your-coding-skills-with-kanos-150-diy-computer-kit) (running a graphical Debian-based OS) powered by a Raspberry Pi 3. Since then, I've grown and improved my skills in programming and Linux. Having become involved with the SBC community in projects like TwisterOS and MCPI-Revival. Currently, I'm looking forward to advancing my knowledge of embedded systems through personal projects involving microcontrollers and PCBs.
