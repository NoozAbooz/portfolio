My first experience with Linux was when I played with the <abbr title="Powered by a Raspberry Pi 3">Kano Computing Kit</abbr> (running a graphical Debian-based OS). Since then, I've become involved with the SBC community in projects like [TwisterOS](https://twisteros.com/ "Desktop distribution for the RPI with themes") and [MCPI-Revival](https://mcpi-revival.github.io/ "Modding project for Minecraft: Pi Edition"). Currently, I'm looking forward to advancing my knowledge of embedded systems through personal projects involving microcontrollers and electrical design.
